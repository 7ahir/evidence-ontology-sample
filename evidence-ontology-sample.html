Design rules

  • AI assesses, people attest. The vocabulary forbids wording such as “AI-certified” or “sensor-proven”.
  • Truth anchor: each fact traces to the accountable source closest to it.
  • Smallest reliable context: agents receive a ContextPacket of cited claims, not whole repositories.
  • Decision trace: retrieved context, tool calls, policy checks, citations and approvals are recorded for audit.
  • One name, one meaning: extend the glossary rather than invent new terms in code.

Status. Entity names and field conventions are used in the prototype’s agent prompts, JSON schemas and data models. The relationships above are the evidence-graph design and are not yet implemented as a graph store. Finance-specific entity types are omitted from this extract.
